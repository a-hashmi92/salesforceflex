Sales Cloud

Sales Cloud revamp for a B2B manufacturer

The problem

Reps tracked deals in spreadsheets alongside a half-configured Salesforce org, so pipeline reports could not be trusted.

What I did

Redesigned the lead, opportunity and quote objects, added Flow-based approvals, and migrated 40k legacy records.

Results

  • Quote turnaround down 70%
  • Single source of truth for pipeline
  • Adoption above 90% in 8 weeks

Tools used

Sales Cloud, Flow, CPQ, Data Loader
